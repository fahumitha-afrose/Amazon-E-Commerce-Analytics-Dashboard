**Amazon E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard**

**Project Overview**
This project presents an end-to-end Power BI analytics dashboard built using Amazon-style e-commerce data.
The dashboard provides actionable insights into product performance, pricing strategy, discounts, and inventory risks to support data-driven business decisions.

**Business Objectives**
Identify top-performing and underperforming products
Evaluate discount and pricing effectiveness
Analyze pricing competitiveness
Detect inventory issues and potential revenue loss
Highlight products requiring immediate action

**Key Insights Covered**
Product and brand performance analysis
Discount impact on ratings and demand
Pricing gaps between actual and discounted prices
Stock availability and out-of-stock risks
High-demand products causing potential revenue loss

**Dashboard Pages**
**1. Product & Brand Performance**
Top-rated and low-rated products
Brand performance and popularity
Brand-level stock availability

**2️. Pricing & Discount Analysis**
Discount vs rating relationship
Discount strategy effectiveness
Actual vs discounted price comparison
Identification of loss-making discounts

**3️. Inventory & Actionable Insights**
Overall stock health
High-demand out-of-stock products
Products requiring immediate action
Category-wise stock risk analysis

**Tools & Technologies**
Power BI – Data visualization and reporting
DAX – KPI and measure creation
Python (Pandas) – Data cleaning and preprocessing
Excel / CSV – Data handling

**Data Preparation**
Cleaned and preprocessed raw e-commerce data
Removed irrelevant columns and handled missing values
**Created derived columns such as:**
Price Difference
Rating Category
Discount Flag
Sales Category
